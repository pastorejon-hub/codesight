Field calculator · Voltage drop (K-factor)

Choose the material and conductor size, then enter the field measurements. Results update as you type. K is an approximate 75°C resistivity constant; verify actual conditions.

Copper K ≈ 12.9; aluminum K ≈ 21.2 at approximately 75°C. Edit K when verified project data calls for another value. This estimates voltage drop only; it does not establish conductor ampacity or compliance.

The 3% and 5% values are commonly used NEC informational-note design recommendations, not universal enforceable pass/fail limits. For 5%, include upstream voltage drop when assessing total feeder plus branch circuit.

Field calculator · Transformer fault-current screening

Approximate maximum symmetrical secondary fault current at transformer terminals, assuming an infinite primary source. Not a service-equipment AFC study or series-rating verification.

Obtain utility fault-current data and account for feeder impedance, motor contributions, equipment ratings and listed series combinations before making compliance decisions.
